Mangoes are among the world’s most beloved tropical fruits, 
believed to have originated in South Asia, particularly the 
region around northeastern India, Bangladesh, and Myanmar.
 Cultivated for thousands of years, mangoes gradually spread 
 across Asia and later reached Africa, Europe, and the Americas 
 through trade and exploration. Their sweet flavor, fragrant aroma,
  and juicy texture made them popular in both traditional cuisine and 
  modern food products. Mango drinks became especially popular as people
 discovered that the fruit could be blended into refreshing juices,
 shakes, smoothies, and sweetened beverages. Commercial production 
 and improved preservation methods eventually allowed mango drinks 
 to become widely available worldwide, transforming mango from a 
 seasonal fruit into a year-round beverage favorite.
