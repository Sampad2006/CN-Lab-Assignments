**Case 1: Error detected by both CRC-8 and Checksum**
- Original Checksum: 0xF543, Corrupted Checksum: 0xF747 (Detected: Yes)
- Original CRC-8: 0x42, Corrupted CRC-8: 0xEB (Detected: Yes)
  Original Frame (first 20 bytes): 66 B9 6A 71 1A 00 8F F7 D9 C8 C9 07 2C 00 1A 15 60 BC E2 CA 
  Corrupted Frame (first 20 bytes): 66 B9 6A 71 1A 00 8F F7 D9 C8 C9 07 2C 00 1A 15 60 BC E2 CA 

**Case 2: Error detected by Checksum but NOT by CRC-8**
- Original Checksum: 0x6D7A, Corrupted Checksum: 0x5D76 (Detected: Yes)
- Original CRC-8: 0x5D, Corrupted CRC-8: 0x5D (Detected: No - Collision!)
  Original Frame (first 20 bytes): 74 A1 68 72 5C 00 40 2D 8F C0 B1 42 2C 00 2E AD CB 3C 1A B7 
  Corrupted Frame (first 20 bytes): 74 A1 68 72 5C 00 40 2D 8F C0 B1 42 2C 00 2E AD CB 3C 1A B7
